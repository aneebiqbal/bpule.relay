## CRITICAL: Evidence Ledger Scoring Dimension Artifacts

**191 out of 191** scored leads have evidence ledger entries that are actually
scoring dimension labels, not real evidence. This is a fundamental design flaw.

### Pattern
Evidence entries like:
- "Opportunity Fit" (STRONG_INFERENCE)
- "Remote Eligibility" (STRONG_INFERENCE)
- "Need / Intent" (STRONG_INFERENCE)
- "Revenue Identity Fit" (STRONG_INFERENCE)
- "Proof Strength" (STRONG_INFERENCE)
- "Access / Reachability" (STRONG_INFERENCE)
- "Timing" (STRONG_INFERENCE)
- "Conversion Evidence" (STRONG_INFERENCE)

These are scoring dimensions being stored as evidence. This pollutes the evidence
ledger and makes it impossible to distinguish actual evidence from scoring metadata.

### Impact
- **Leads**: 190/191 (99.5%) affected
- **Captured prospects**: 746/753 (99.1%) affected
